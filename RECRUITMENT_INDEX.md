## Key Messages

**Elevator Pitch:**
"Peer review platform that pays reviewers $20-40/month, uses AI to match papers to specialists, and provides modern tools. Authors pay $100/month subscription. Net cost: $60-80/month for dramatically better experience."

**Value Props:**
1. Fair compensation ($20-40/month for reviews)
2. Smart AI matching (only relevant papers)
3. Modern tools (real-time collaboration, not email/PDF)

**Top Objection:**
"Why pay $100 to earn $40?"
→ "You're paying for fast expert reviews on YOUR papers + earning for reviewing others. Net: $60/month for better experience both ways."
