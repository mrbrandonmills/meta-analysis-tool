# Incentive Structure for Beta Recruitment

## Overview
Beyond the core $20-40/month compensation, we need additional incentives to attract and retain the first 10 researchers for proof of concept. This document outlines a comprehensive incentive strategy.

---

## Core Economic Incentives

### 1. Reduced First Month Cost
**Incentive:** 50% off first month
**Value:** $50 savings
**Why it works:** Lowers barrier to entry, reduces risk
**Implementation:** Promo code at signup: FOUNDING10

### 2. Lifetime Discount
**Incentive:** 20% off forever (after month 1)
**Value:** $20/month × 12 months = $240/year savings
**Cumulative value:** $2,400 over 10 years
**Why it works:** Long-term value, reward for early adoption
**Implementation:** "Founding Reviewer" account status with permanent discount

### 3. Bonus for Early Completion
**Incentive:** $50 bonus for completing onboarding within 48 hours
**Value:** $50 one-time
**Why it works:** Urgency, reduces drop-off, faster time to value
**Implementation:** Automated bonus if onboarding completed ≤48 hours from signup

### 4. Referral Bonuses
**Incentive:** $50 for each researcher you recruit who completes onboarding
**Value:** $50 per referral (potential $450 if they recruit 9 others)
**Why it works:** Network effects, leverages social proof, trusted recommendations
**Implementation:** Unique referral links, bonus paid after referee completes first review

### 5. Performance Bonuses
**Incentive:** $25 bonus for exceptional reviews
**Value:** $25 per exceptional review (max 1/month)
**Criteria:**
- Author rates review as "exceptional" (5/5 stars)
- Review completed within 10 days
- Detailed, constructive feedback (>500 words)
**Why it works:** Incentivizes quality, not just quantity
**Implementation:** Author rating system + automated bonus

## Incentive Communication Strategy

### At Initial Outreach
Mention:
- 50% off first month
- Lifetime 20% discount
- Founding Reviewer status

Keep it simple. Don't overwhelm.

### After Expression of Interest
Reveal:
- Referral bonuses ($50 per recruit)
- Early access to features
- Certificate of completion

Build excitement and show depth of benefits.

### After Signup Completion
Unveil:
- Performance bonuses ($25 for exceptional reviews)
- Co-authorship opportunity (for active users)
- All remaining incentives

Maximize retention and engagement.

### Throughout Beta
Remind:
- Progress toward co-authorship threshold
- Referral link and current earnings
- Upcoming office hours
- New features being released

Keep engagement high.

## Anti-Incentive (What NOT to Offer)

### Don't Offer:
1. **Free subscription** - Breaks the economic model we're testing
2. **Guaranteed income** - Reviews must be quality-dependent
3. **Cash upfront** - Reduces commitment, increases drop-out
4. **Over-the-top gifts** - Cheapens the professional relationship
5. **Equity** - Too complex for beta testers, raises expectations

### Why These Don't Work:
- Free subscription = Can't test if people will actually pay
- Guaranteed income = Removes quality incentive
- Cash upfront = Sunk cost fallacy works in reverse
- Gifts = Feels transactional, not professional
- Equity = Legal complexity, misaligned expectations

## Incentive Budget

### Per Beta Tester (Year 1)

**Guaranteed:**
- $50 (first month 50% off)
- $240 (lifetime 20% discount × 12 months)
- $0 (non-monetary: badge, certificate, access)

**Subtotal:** $290/tester

**Variable:**
- $0-450 (referral bonuses, if they recruit others)
- $0-300 (performance bonuses, $25 × up to 12 exceptional reviews)
- $0-500 (conference travel, if selected for presentation)

**Potential Total:** $290-1,540/tester

### Total Budget (10 Beta Testers)

**Guaranteed:** $2,900
**Variable (high-end):** $12,500
**Total Budget:** $15,400 for 10 beta testers (year 1)

**ROI:**
- 10 testers × $1,200/year revenue = $12,000/year
- Net cost year 1: -$3,400
- Net revenue year 2+: +$12,000/year (if they stay)
- Break-even: 3.3 months into year 2

**If retention is 80% over 3 years:**
- Revenue: 8 testers × $1,200/year × 3 years = $28,800
- Incentive cost (discounts): 8 × $720 (3 years at 20% off) = $5,760
- Net revenue: $23,040

**Conclusion:** High upfront investment, strong long-term ROI if we retain beta testers.

## Legal & Ethical Considerations

### Tax Implications
- All payments >$600/year require 1099 form (US)
- Researchers must report as income
- Platform must collect W-9 (US) or equivalent

### Disclosure
- Be transparent about incentive structure
- No hidden fees or surprise costs
- Clear terms of service

### Fairness
- All beta testers get same base incentives
- Performance bonuses are merit-based and objective
- No favoritism in feature access or support

### Privacy
- Don't publicly share earnings without consent
- Anonymize data when discussing case studies
- Respect confidentiality

## Summary: Incentive Strategy

**Goal:** Recruit 10 high-quality beta testers and keep them engaged for 6+ months.

**Approach:** Multi-layered incentives appealing to different motivations:
1. **Economic:** Discounts, bonuses, referrals
2. **Status:** Badges, recognition, exclusivity
3. **Professional:** Publications, certificates, recommendations
4. **Social:** Community, networking, influence
5. **Intrinsic:** Better tools, time savings, data insights

**Budget:** ~$15,400 for 10 testers (year 1), break-even by month 3 of year 2

**Success Metrics:**
- 10 researchers recruited (10/10)
- 80%+ onboarding completion (8/10)
- 60%+ active at 3 months (6/10)
- 50%+ active at 6 months (5/10)
- At least 1 referral generated by beta testers

**Key Insight:** Diversified incentives appeal to different personality types. Money motivates some, status motivates others, professional development motivates a third group. By offering all three, we maximize appeal and retention.
