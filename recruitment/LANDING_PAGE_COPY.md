## FAQ Section

### Section Headline
**Frequently Asked Questions**

### Q: How much can I earn per month?
**A:** You can earn $20-40/month depending on reviews completed:
- 2 reviews = $20
- 3 reviews = $30
- 4 reviews = $40
Maximum 4 reviews/month to ensure quality.

### Q: Why do reviewers pay a subscription?
**A:** The $100/month subscription allows you to submit your own papers for review. Think of it as:
- Pay $100 to get fast, quality reviews on your papers
- Earn $20-40 by reviewing others' papers
- Net cost: $60-80/month for a better peer review experience

If you only want to review (not submit papers), we'll have a reviewer-only tier in Phase 2.

### Q: How long does each review take?
**A:** Average 1.5-2 hours per review, depending on paper complexity. Total time commitment: 3-4 hours for 2 reviews, 6-8 hours for 4 reviews per month.

### Q: What if I'm too busy one month?
**A:** You control how many reviews you accept. Skip a month if needed - no penalties. Your subscription continues (for submitting papers), but you simply won't have reviewer earnings that month.

### Q: Can I cancel anytime?
**A:** Yes. Cancel anytime with no penalties. You'll have access through the end of your billing period.

### Q: How do payouts work?
**A:** Payouts are processed via Stripe in the first week of each month for previous month's completed reviews. You can receive payments via direct deposit or debit card.

### Q: What papers will I review?
**A:** Only papers matched to your expertise based on:
- Your publication history
- Google Scholar profile analysis
- Self-reported specializations
- Past review performance

You can decline any assignment if it's not a good fit.

### Q: Is my data secure?
**A:** Yes. We use:
- Bank-level encryption (256-bit SSL)
- Secure payment processing (Stripe - PCI compliant)
- Anonymous review options (if preferred)
- GDPR compliance for EU users
- No data selling (ever)

### Q: Who else is on the platform?
**A:** We're starting with 10 beta testers (psychology researchers with H-index 10+). After proving the concept, we'll expand to more fields and more users.

### Q: What if a review is low quality?
**A:** We have quality control measures:
- Detailed onboarding ensures qualified reviewers
- Review history tracking
- Reputation system (coming in Phase 2)
- Authors can flag concerns
- Repeated low-quality reviews may result in removal

### Q: How is this different from Publons/Web of Science?
**A:** Publons tracks your review contributions but doesn't pay you. We compensate reviewers directly and provide a modern review workflow (not just PDF annotation).

### Q: Do I need to be a professor?
**A:** No. We welcome:
- Professors (all ranks)
- Postdocs
- Research scientists
- Independent researchers
- Anyone with strong publication record (H-index 10+ preferred)

### Q: What about conflicts of interest?
**A:** Our AI matching algorithm avoids:
- Papers from your institution
- Co-authors you've published with
- Close collaborators (based on citation networks)
- Papers you've cited in the last 2 years

You can also manually decline any assignment.

### Q: Can I review more than 4 papers/month?
**A:** Not initially. We cap at 4 reviews/month to ensure quality. After we prove the model works, we may increase limits.

## Design Notes

### Brand Voice
- Professional but not stuffy
- Enthusiastic but not hype-y
- Data-driven but accessible
- Academic credibility + startup energy

### Color Palette Suggestions
- Primary: Trust blue (#2563EB) or Academic purple (#7C3AED)
- Secondary: Success green (#10B981) for earnings/payments
- Accent: Warning amber (#F59E0B) for urgency ("7 spots left")
- Neutral: Clean grays for text/backgrounds

### Typography
- Headlines: Bold, sans-serif (Inter, Poppins, or similar)
- Body: Readable, generous line height (1.6-1.8)
- CTAs: Heavy weight, high contrast

### Layout
- Clean, spacious (white space is good)
- Clear visual hierarchy
- F-pattern layout (readers scan left-to-right)
- Progressive disclosure (detail on demand)

### Imagery
