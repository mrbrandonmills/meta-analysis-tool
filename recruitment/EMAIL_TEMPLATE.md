## Response Templates

### If They Ask About Time Commitment

"Great question. Time breakdown:
- Onboarding: 15 minutes (one-time)
- Review matching: Automatic (AI does this)
- Per review: 1.5-2 hours average
- Monthly total: 3-4 hours for 2 reviews, 6-8 hours for 4 reviews

You control how many reviews you accept each month. Start with 2, scale up if you want."

### If They Ask About Payment Details

"Payment structure:
- Reviews completed: 2 = $20, 3 = $30, 4 = $40
- Payout method: Stripe (direct deposit or debit card)
- Payout timing: First week of following month
- Minimum: $20 (complete at least 2 reviews)

Example: Complete 3 reviews in December → Get $30 in early January."

### If They Ask "Why Would I Pay $100 to Earn $40?"

"Fair question. Two ways to think about it:

**Option 1: You're already submitting papers**
If you submit papers that need reviews, you're paying $100/month to get faster, better reviews from qualified experts. The $20-40 you earn from reviewing offsets the cost.

**Option 2: Reviewer-only mode (coming in Phase 2)**
If you only want to review, we'll have a reviewer-only tier (no subscription, just payouts). But beta testers need to be authors too so we can test the full ecosystem.

Bottom line: This is for active researchers who both submit AND review papers."

### If They Ask About Paper Volume

"For proof of concept:
- You'll submit 1-2 papers per month for review
- You'll review 2-4 papers per month
- Papers are matched from other beta testers initially
- After beta, papers come from broader user base

We need the two-sided marketplace to function properly."
